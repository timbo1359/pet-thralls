# Pet Thralls

Turn your Arceuus thrall into one of your pets.

![A greater zombified thrall next to the same thrall shown as a Hellpuppy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/timbo1359/pet-thralls/master/images/thrall-to-pet.png)

Pet Thralls swaps the look of **your own** thrall for any pet you've unlocked in your collection log. It's purely cosmetic and your thrall fights exactly the same as before. Other players see a normal thrall, unless they're in your RuneLite party and use Pet Thralls too: with the **Party pets** option, you see each other's thralls as each other's pets.

## Features

- **Any pet you own.** Every pet in the collection log can be used, including Orion, checked against your collection log so only the pets you've actually got are allowed.
- **Every look.** Once you own a pet, all of its alternate looks are available too: Phoenix colours, rock golem ores, Olmlet's raid looks, the Tombs of Amascut guardians, Robed Orion and more.
- **A pet for each thrall.** Use one pet for every thrall, or pick a different pet for your mage, range and melee thralls.
- **Random pet.** Choose *Random unlocked pet* and each thrall you summon becomes a different one of your pets, in any of its looks.
- **Only your thrall.** Other players' thralls stay as they are, even in raids and busy worlds, unless they're party members sharing their pet.
- **Party pets.** In a RuneLite party, you and your party members who also use Pet Thralls see each other's thralls as each other's pets. Each player's own plugin says which thrall is theirs, so it's never guessed.
- **Moves like the pet.** The pet follows your thrall around, walks and idles with its own animations, and attacks whenever your thrall does: a magic attack for a ghostly thrall, ranged for skeletal and melee for zombified, when the pet has one.
- **Keeps the thrall's markings.** The pet stands on your thrall's glowing symbol and is outlined in its colour, so you can still tell at a glance which thrall you have out.

## Getting started

1. Install **Pet Thralls** from the Plugin Hub.
2. Open **Collection log > Other > All Pets** once, so the plugin can see which pets you have. It remembers them for each account and picks up new pets automatically. Orion isn't on the All Pets page, so if you have it, open its own page (**The Fractured Archive**) too.
3. In the plugin's settings, choose a pet. Pets you've unlocked are shown in **green** and the rest in **red**. Each pet's other looks are listed underneath it. For a different pet per thrall type, open **Pet for each thrall type**, tick **Use these pets** and choose one for each.
4. Cast any Resurrect Thrall spell.
5. Optional: to see your friends' pets and let them see yours, join the same RuneLite party from the Party panel. Everyone needs Pet Thralls with **Party pets** turned on.

![The Pet Thralls settings, and the pet list showing unlocked pets in green](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/timbo1359/pet-thralls/master/images/settings.png)

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pet** | The pet your thrall appears as. *Random unlocked pet* picks a new one each time you summon a thrall. Choose *None (normal thrall)* to turn it off. |
| **Pet for each thrall type** | Tick **Use these pets** to choose a separate pet (or Random, or None) for each thrall type: **Mage** for ghostly, **Range** for skeletal and **Melee** for zombified. These replace the **Pet** setting while ticked. |
| **Thrall symbol** | Shows the thrall's glowing symbol on the ground under the pet. |
| **Thrall outline** | Outlines the pet in the thrall's colour: purple for ghostly, green for skeletal and red for zombified. |
| **Party pets** | In a RuneLite party, shares your pet with party members who use Pet Thralls and shows their thralls as their pets. On by default. |

## Good to know

- If you choose a pet you haven't unlocked, your thrall stays normal and a chat message tells you why. If you've only just got the pet, open the All Pets page (or the pet's own page) to update the plugin.
- A random pet stays the same for as long as that thrall is out, and is never the same pet twice in a row. Party members see the same pick.
- A few pets have no attack animation in the game (rock golems, most beavers, baby chinchompas, Lil' Bloat and Dom), so they stay idle while your thrall attacks.
- See-through parts of a pet, such as Youngllef's crystals, aren't outlined.
- Party pets need everyone to be in the same RuneLite party (from the Party panel) with **Party pets** turned on. Only your chosen pet and which thrall is yours are shared, through RuneLite's own party server. Your symbol and outline settings apply to the pets you see.
- The pictures above are rendered from the game's own models.
